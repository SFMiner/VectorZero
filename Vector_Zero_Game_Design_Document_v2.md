## 1. High Concept

### 1.1 Logline

**An evolutionary action-adventure where players transform from a single point into complex geometric forms across 24+ stages spanning four acts. The game's physics, puzzles, and story are direct manifestations of mathematical principles—from basic geometry through calculus to 3D transcendence—creating an experience of "learning through feeling" rather than instruction.**

### 1.2 Core Information

- **Genre:** Action-Adventure, Puzzle, Educational (Edutainment)
- **Target Platforms:** 
  - Primary: Windows, macOS, Linux (via executable)
  - Secondary: HTML5 (browser-based for Acts I-II)
- **Target Audience:** 
  - Primary: Curious players ages 12+, students (middle school through college)
  - Secondary: Math enthusiasts, puzzle game fans, players who enjoy Journey/Monument Valley
  - Tertiary: Educators seeking engaging math visualization tools

### 1.3 Unique Selling Points

1. **Evolutionary Gameplay:** Player form fundamentally changes 20+ times, each transformation altering abilities and physics
2. **Embedded Learning:** Mathematical concepts are the physical laws of the game world, not quiz questions
3. **Narrative Geometry:** Story of connection and growth told through geometric transformations
4. **The Uncatchable Companion:** The Differential exists in change itself—a character you can never meet but always feel
5. **Character-Driven Mathematics:** Each concept (Pi, Epsilon, The Integral) is a personality with voice and motivation
6. **Educational Without Being Educational:** Calculus through movement, algebra through puzzles, no textbooks
7. **Dimensional Transcendence:** Journey from 2D to 3D, experiencing dimension shift viscerally
8. **Multiple Endings:** Four distinct conclusions reflecting player philosophy
9. **Optional Depth:** Advanced sub-worlds (Fractals, Topology, Set Theory, Statistics) for mastery players
10. **Zero-Death Challenge:** Complete the game without ever "zeroing out" via inverse collision

### 1.4 Inspirations

- **Gameplay:** Journey (emotional arc), Monument Valley (geometry), The Witness (environmental learning)
- **Aesthetics:** Tron (neon minimalism), Rez (synesthesia), Geometry Wars (vector graphics)
- **Educational:** DragonBox (implicit learning), Euclidea (geometric discovery)
- **Narrative:** Everything (philosophical scale), Katamari Damacy (transformation joy)

## 2. Core Themes & Philosophy

### 2.1 Primary Themes

1. **Connection Creates Capability:** Individual parts are limited; unity unlocks potential
2. **Mathematical Consciousness:** The game world is mathematics experiencing itself
3. **Identity Through Limitation:** Boundaries define us, transcendence transforms us
4. **Understanding Through Experience:** Feel first, name later—intuition before formalization

### 2.2 Design Pillars

1. **Experience Over Explanation:** No tutorials, lectures, or exposition—only play
2. **Form Follows Function:** Each shape's abilities emerge naturally from its geometric properties
3. **Emotional Mathematics:** Attach feelings to abstract concepts through character and narrative
4. **Layered Comprehension:** Middle schoolers experience mystery; college students recognize formulas
5. **Respectful Challenge:** Difficulty scales with understanding; hints available but never forced

### 2.3 The "Learning Through Feeling" Philosophy

**Traditional Math Education:**
- Formula → Memorization → Application → (Maybe) Understanding

**Vector Zero's Approach:**
- Experience → Pattern Recognition → Emotional Attachment → (Later) Formal Understanding

**Example:**
- A student plays Stage 13 (The Differential)
- Experiences frustration trying to "catch" change
- Feels the uncatchable nature of instantaneous velocity
- Years later in Calculus class: "OH! This is what the Differential was!"
- The derivative is no longer abstract—it's an old friend

## 3. Story & Narrative

### 3.1 Complete Story Arc Summary

**Act I: ASSEMBLY** (Stages 1-6)  
*Theme: From isolation to connection*

Vector Zero breaks free from the origin (0,0), discovers Echo, forms a line segment, meets skeptical Axiom, struggles as an unstable angle, and finally unites as the first triangle—capturing the Heartlight at their center.

**Act II: DIVERSITY** (Stages 7-11)  
*Theme: Understanding through diversity and mathematical foundations*

The group adds vertices (quadrilaterals, pentagons, circles), meets variables (X, Y, Z), learns functions and graphing, discovers ratios (Phi), and experiences the infinite through Pi.

**Act III: DEPTH** (Stages 12-18)  
*Theme: Breaking and remaking assumptions*

Encounters trigonometry (Sin/Cos/Tan), meets The Differential (uncatchable companion), experiences Epsilon's infinitesimals, learns integration from The Integral, confronts The Inverse (shadow self), masters transformations, and navigates non-Euclidean space.

**Act IV: TRANSCENDENCE** (Stages 19-24)  
*Theme: Recognizing true nature*

Meets i (imaginary unit), discovers the third dimension, navigates gradient fields, transforms through Platonic solids, becomes a sphere, returns to origin (0,0,0), and chooses final form: The Cycle, The Teacher, The Unity, or The Explorer.

**Optional Sub-Worlds:**
- Statistics & Probability (Bell, Mean/Median/Mode)
- Set Theory (Empty Set, Venn diagrams)
- Fractals (Benoit, self-similarity)
- Topology (Möbius, continuous deformation)

### 3.2 Death/Rebirth Mechanic

**The Inversion:**
- Touching your inverse self returns player to origin (0,0) or (0,0,0)
- **Knowledge Persistence:** All puzzle solutions and mathematical understanding retained
- **Physical Loss:** Companions and abilities must be regathered
- **Narrative Framing:** Not death but "zeroing out"—return to potential

**Other Disappearances:**
- **Cancellation:** +3 and -3 touching = both vanish (sum to zero)
- **Negative Realm:** Objects falling into negative zones become negative equivalents
- **Not destruction:** Mathematical transformations, not disappearance

**Optional Challenge:**
- Complete game without ever touching inverse = "Zero-Death Run"
- Achievement: "Never Zero"

### 3.3 Emotional Beats

**Stage 1:** Loneliness → Liberation  
**Stage 3:** First connection → Joy  
**Stage 4:** Conflict → Compromise  
**Stage 6:** Unity → Triumph  
**Stage 9:** Discovery of functions → "We are the graph!"  
**Stage 11:** Infinite → Wonder  
**Stage 13:** Meeting The Differential → Frustration → Acceptance  
**Stage 16:** Confronting Inverse → Fear → Understanding  
**Stage 20:** Third dimension revealed → Revelation  
**Stage 23:** Becoming sphere → Peace  
**Stage 24:** Final choice → Player-driven conclusion  

### 3.4 Thematic Questions

1. Do we lose ourselves when we connect, or become more?
2. Is complexity better than simplicity?
3. Are boundaries prisons or definitions?
4. What realities exist beyond our perception?
5. Can we ever leave our origin, or does it always define us?
6. What is the nature of change itself?
7. How does connection create strength?
8. Can we truly know the infinite and irrational?

## 4. Characters

### 4.1 Primary Characters (The Core)

#### **Vector Zero (The Player)**
- **Role:** Protagonist, catalyst for all connections
- **Arc:** Lonely point → Connected line → Leader → Complex form → Infinite → Complete
- **Voice:** Curious, driven, empathetic
- **Visual:** Cyan glowing point/shape (player-controlled)

#### **Echo**
- **Role:** First companion, emotional voice
- **Arc:** Cautious → Protective → Accepting → Integrated
- **Personality:** Intuitive, values bonds, initially resistant to change
- **Voice Persists:** "This is magnificent" (beauty), "We're together" (danger)
- **Visual:** Magenta glowing point

#### **Axiom**
- **Role:** Second companion, logical voice
- **Arc:** Skeptical → Logical → Understanding → Integrated
- **Personality:** Analytical, pattern-seeking, needs proof
- **Voice Persists:** "The pattern suggests..." (analysis), "It was always true" (revelation)
- **Visual:** Yellow glowing point

#### **The Heartlight**
- **Role:** External objective → Internal ability → Player's essence
- **Arc:** Alone → Captured → Tool → Self
- **Evolution:**
  - Stage 6: Captured at incenter
  - Stage 11: Becomes insight ability
  - Stage 15: Mode switcher (derivative/integral)
  - Stage 21: Gradient compass
  - Stage 23: IS the player (fills entire sphere)
- **Visual:** Warm white/gold glow, changes color by mode

### 4.2 Mathematical Entities (Act II)

#### **X (Independent Variable)**
- **Role:** Sentient variable, can hold any value
- **Personality:** Free-spirited, adaptable
- **Key Line:** "I can be anything. I vary."
- **Visual:** Green glowing container showing current value

#### **Y (Dependent Variable/Function)**
- **Role:** Becomes first function (Y = X + 3)
- **Personality:** Responsive, purposeful
- **Arc:** Variable → Function → Graph-maker
- **Key Line:** "I function to add 3 to X. I am a function of X."
- **Visual:** Orange container, leaves graph trails when moving

#### **Z (Unknown Constant)**
- **Role:** Appears as variable but is always 3
- **Personality:** Silent until revelation
- **Key Line:** "I wore the label of a variable, but I was never variable. I was always constant. Always 3."
- **Visual:** Gray container (unknown) → White (revealed as +3)

#### **Phi (φ) - Golden Ratio**
- **Role:** Proportion embodied
- **Personality:** Aesthetic, harmonious
- **Key Line:** "I appear in growth, in spirals, in beauty. I am proportion itself."
- **Visual:** Golden spiral overlay on rectangles
- **Value:** ≈ 1.618...

#### **Sigma (Σ) - Summation**
- **Role:** Gatherer of sequences
- **Personality:** Collective, additive
- **Key Line:** "I gather all the pieces together. I add infinitely many things."
- **Visual:** Stacked notation symbol
- **Voice Persists:** "We grow stronger together"

#### **Delta (Δ) - Change**
- **Role:** Transformation itself
- **Personality:** Fluid, evolutionary
- **Key Line:** "You approach something infinite"
- **Visual:** Triangle morphing symbol
- **Voice Persists:** "We are becoming"

### 4.3 Transcendent Beings (Act III)

#### **Pi (π)**
- **Role:** The infinite, the irrational
- **Personality:** Mysterious, never fully knowable
- **Key Line:** "I cannot be fully known, only approximated. Infinity contains all things."
- **Visual:** Circular aura, endless decimal digits flowing
- **Value:** 3.14159...

#### **Sin, Cos, Tan (Trigonometric Functions)**
- **Sin:** Vertical wave rider, rises and falls eternally
- **Cos:** Horizontal wave rider, phase-shifted from Sin
- **Tan:** Slope incarnate, grows without bound
- **Key Line (Sin/Cos):** "We are the circle, expressed through time."
- **Key Line (Tan):** "At 90 degrees, I am infinite. Undefined. I am the vertical itself."
- **Visual:** Undulating wave forms

#### **The Differential (d/dx)**
- **Role:** The uncatchable companion, exists in change
- **Personality:** Paradoxical, profound, always ahead/behind
- **Key Line:** "You exist at points. I exist between them. I am the change that you will be in the world."
- **Mechanics:** Communicates through velocity-based messages, oscillation conversations
- **Visual:** Glowing traces, velocity vectors, message particles
- **Educational Impact:** Most memorable character—teaches derivatives through impossibility

#### **Epsilon (ε) - The Infinitely Small**
- **Role:** Infinitesimal guide
- **Personality:** Quiet, paradoxical
- **Key Line:** "I am smaller than small, yet I am not nothing."
- **Visual:** Nearly invisible shimmer
- **Value:** Approaches 0

#### **The Integral (∫)**
- **Role:** Accumulator, gatherer of infinitesimals
- **Personality:** Patient, comprehensive
- **Key Line:** "I remember all changes and sum them. I gather all the pieces."
- **Visual:** Elongated S symbol, collecting particles
- **Relationship:** Inverse of The Differential

### 4.4 Dimensional Guides (Acts III-IV)

#### **i (Imaginary Unit)**
- **Role:** Bridge to complex plane and 3D
- **Personality:** Perpendicular, orthogonal to reality
- **Key Line:** "Imaginary is a misnomer. I am as real as you. I simply exist in a direction you haven't moved yet."
- **Mechanics:** Multiplication by i = 90° rotation
- **Visual:** Perpendicular axis, phase-shifted appearance
- **Value:** √-1

#### **The Gradient (∇)**
- **Role:** Optimizer, steepest path finder
- **Personality:** Directional, purposeful
- **Key Line:** "I point always toward the steepest ascent. Follow me to find maxima. Oppose me to find minima."
- **Mechanics:** Vector field navigation, optimization puzzles
- **Visual:** Arrow field, Heartlight becomes compass
- **Appears:** Act IV (3D)

#### **Euler**
- **Role:** Pattern keeper, relationship revealer
- **Personality:** Precise, lawful
- **Key Line:** "V - E + F = 2. Always. This is law. This is truth."
- **Visual:** Formula overlay on polyhedra

### 4.5 The Inverse

#### **The Inverse (Additive Inverse)**

- **Visual:** Perfect mirror of player, inverted colors

## 5. Gameplay & Mechanics

### 5.1 Core Gameplay Loop

```
Encounter Obstacle 
    ↓
Recognize Current Form's Limitation
    ↓
Discover New Companion/Transformation
    ↓
Transform (Gain New Abilities)
    ↓
Overcome Obstacle
    ↓
Experience Mathematical Concept Mechanically
    ↓
Internalize Understanding
    ↓
[LOOP]
```

### 5.2 Act I Mechanics (Stages 1-6)

#### **Stage 1: The Point**
**Form:** Single point (Vector Zero)

**Controls:**
- Movement: WASD / Arrow Keys / Left Stick
- Dash: Spacebar / A Button

**Abilities:**
- **Move:** Fluid, omnidirectional 2D movement
- **Dash:** Short burst of speed

**Mechanics:**
- Navigate mazes of lines and curves
- Coordinate display always visible: (X, Y)
- Grid pulses at integer coordinates
- Pure exploration, no enemies

**Educational Concepts:**
- Cartesian coordinates (passive exposure)
- Position on a plane
- Basic navigation

**Puzzle Example:**
- Maze requiring precise positioning to slip through gaps

---

#### **Stage 2: The Wanderer**
**Form:** Still a point

**New Abilities:**
- Enhanced dash distance

**Mechanics:**
- Distance measurement visualization appears
- Obstacles requiring "reach" are visible but insurmountable
- See Echo in distance, cannot reach across gap

**Educational Concepts:**
- Distance
- Magnitude vs. position
- Limitations of a point

**Puzzle Example:**
- Wide chasm with Echo on far side, unreachable by point alone

---

#### **Stage 3: The Line Segment**
**Form:** Two points connected (Vector Zero + Echo)

**Controls:**
- **Unity Mode (Default):** Move as single rigid body
- **Toggle Endpoint Mode:** Hold Shift / Left Trigger
  - While held: Anchor one endpoint
  - Other endpoint pivots around anchor
  - Can change length and orientation

**Abilities:**
- Span gaps (bridge)
- Act as lever (pivot under objects to lift)
- Fit into length-specific slots

**Mechanics:**
- Length measurement displayed
- Slope indicator when static
- Can rotate by pivoting endpoints

**Educational Concepts:**
- Length
- Slope
- Linear measurement
- Translation (all movement is sliding)

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Bridge across chasm
- Lever to lift heavy gate
- Fit into slot that requires length = 5 units

---

#### **Stage 4: The Unstable Angle**
**Form:** Three points, Vector Zero as vertex, Echo and Axiom as endpoints (NOT connected to each other)

**Controls:**
- Same as Line Segment, but now TWO arms to manage
- Difficult to control due to floppy physics

**Physics:**
- **Structural Instability:** Arms drag, swing with momentum
- Environmental forces (wind, gravity) affect each arm independently
- Very challenging to maintain precise form

**Mechanics:**
- Angle measurement displayed when static
- Rotation mechanics introduced explicitly
- Struggle against physics is the puzzle

**Educational Concepts:**
- Angles
- Degrees
- Structural instability (open shapes are weak)
- Rotation as transformation

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Navigate narrow passage while fighting environmental "wind"
- Position at specific angle to activate mechanism
- Reach two switches simultaneously with two arms

---

#### **Stage 5: The Heartlight Discovery**
**Form:** Still unstable angle

**Objective:**
- Capture the Heartlight (glowing orb)

**Heartlight Behavior:**
- Moves away from player if approached too fast
- Can only be captured by complete enclosure
- Slips through gap between Echo and Axiom

**Mechanics:**
- Chase sequences
- Failed containment attempts
- Environmental puzzles requiring object containment
- Heartlight glows brighter when near player

**Educational Concepts:**
- Enclosure
- Interior vs. exterior
- Open vs. closed shapes

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Herd Heartlight into corner (fails—escapes through gap)
- Try to pin Heartlight against wall (fails—slips around)
- Environmental puzzle: Move other objects into containers (succeeds, teaching concept)

---

#### **Stage 6: The First Triangle**
**Form:** Closed three-sided polygon (Vector Zero + Echo + Axiom, all connected)

**Transformation:**
- Dramatic visual/audio event
- Floppy physics GONE—now rigid and strong
- Heartlight settles at incenter, glowing warmly

**Abilities:**
- Push heavy objects (rigid body)
- Resist environmental forces
- Act as solid shield
- **Heartlight Ability Unlocked:** See hidden geometric properties (e.g., angle measures, area)

**Mechanics:**
- Rigid body physics
- Can contain objects now
- Area-based puzzles unlock
- Incenter visualization

**Educational Concepts:**
- Structural integrity
- Incenter
- Area of triangles
- Closed shapes are strong

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Push heavy cube to pressure plate
- Shield against projectiles
- Capture Heartlight successfully
- Unlock door requiring triangular key

**Emotional Beat:**
- Echo: "I was wrong. We're stronger together."
- Axiom: "The optimal solution was unity all along."

### 5.3 Act II Mechanics (Stages 7-11)

#### **Stage 7: The Quadrilateral Choices**
**Form:** Four vertices (add Sigma)

**Transformation Mechanic:**
- Same four vertices can form different quadrilaterals
- Player can reshape: Square, Rectangle, Rhombus, Parallelogram, Trapezoid

**Controls:**
- **Shape Selection:** Number keys 1-5 or D-pad + A
- Each shape has unique properties

**Shape Properties:**
| Shape | Stability | Speed | Special Ability |
|-------|-----------|-------|-----------------|
| Square | Highest | Medium | Perfect stacking |
| Rectangle | High | Slow | Wide bridging |
| Rhombus | Medium | Fast | Compression fit |
| Parallelogram | Low | Fast | Slanted sliding |
| Trapezoid | Medium | Medium | Angled support |

**Mechanics:**
- Shape-shifting puzzles
- Each form required for specific challenges
- Heartlight shifts between center types (centroid, circumcenter)

**Educational Concepts:**
- Quadrilateral types
- Parallel lines
- Diagonals
- Same vertices, different arrangements

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Stack square blocks to reach height
- Bridge wide gap with rectangle
- Compress through narrow diagonal passage with rhombus

---

#### **Stage 7b: Ratios and the Golden Rectangle**
**Form:** Special rectangle (Golden Ratio proportions)

**New Character:** Phi (φ)

**Mechanics:**
- **Proportion Lock:** Maintain width:height ratio while scaling
- Golden rectangles unlock special passages
- Visual distortion if proportions are wrong

**Abilities:**
- Scale up/down while maintaining ratio
- Fit into Golden Ratio keyholes

**Educational Concepts:**
- Ratios (a:b)
- Proportions
- Golden Ratio (φ ≈ 1.618)
- Scale factors

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Scale up to reach high platform, maintaining φ ratio
- Fit into Golden Rectangle slot (wrong ratio = blocked)
- Create Golden Spiral by recursive subdivision

---

#### **Stage 8: Meeting Variables**
**Form:** Quadrilateral (or Triangle, player choice)

**New Characters:** X, Y, Z

**Variable System Introduction:**
- Variables appear as glowing containers
- Can hold numerical values
- Values can be changed by player

**Controls:**
- **Assign Value:** Interact with variable, use number keys or slider
- Example: X ← 7 (X now holds value 7)

**Mechanics:**
- Gates that open when X = specific value
- Comparison gates: X > Y, X < 10, etc.
- Variables displayed in UI: `X = 7`, `Y = 10`, `Z = ???`

**Educational Concepts:**
- Variables hold values
- Variables can change ("vary")
- Assignment operation (←)

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Door opens when X = 5
- Bridge extends when Y > X
- Multiple gates requiring X = 3, then X = 7, then X = 12 in sequence

---

#### **Stage 9: The Balance Puzzle (Equations & Solving)**
**Form:** Same as Stage 8

**Key Puzzle:** Balance scale blocking path

**Setup:**
- Left platform: Y (variable)
- Right platform: X (variable) + Z (unknown constant, fixed to scale)
- Scale inscription: **Y = X + Z**
- Scale only balances when equation is true

**Mechanics:**
- Adjust X and Y using variable controls
- Observe scale tipping
- Scale balances when Y = X + Z
- Must solve for Z (Z = 3)

**Solution Methods:**
1. Set X = 0, adjust Y until balanced → Y = 3, therefore Z = 3
2. Set any Y, adjust X until balanced, solve equation

**Z's Revelation:**
- After solving, Z glows and speaks for first time
- Z: "I am 3. I have always been 3."
- Distinction between variables (can vary) and unknown constants (fixed but hidden)

**Educational Concepts:**
- Equations as relationships
- Solving for unknowns
- Balancing equations (what you do to one side, do to the other)
- Variables vs. constants

**Puzzle Example:**
- Balance scale is the puzzle

---

#### **Stage 9b: The Narrow Gate (Functions)**
**Form:** Same

**Key Puzzle:** Gate that only allows ONE entity through

**Problem:**
- Have X, Y, Z
- Need all three on other side
- Gate blocks if carrying multiple variables

**Solution:**
- **Y absorbs X and the constant 3** (or Z)
- Y transforms from variable into function: **Y(X) = X + 3**

**Function Transformation:**
- Visual: Y's container now shows `Y(X) = X + 3` with X and 3 inside
- X remains independent (can still be changed)
- Y is now dependent (automatically updates when X changes)

**Function Graphing Mechanic:**
- **Y's Trail:** When Y moves, it leaves a glowing path
- As X changes value, Y's position updates
- The trail forms a graph of the function
- Visual: Diagonal line for Y = X + 3

**Grid Awareness Moment:**
- Axiom: "The whole grid is made of X and Y positions. We've been navigating through pairs of (X, Y) values this entire time."
- Echo: "We *are* the graph. We've always been on the graph."

**Controls:**
- **Change X:** Number keys / slider
- **Observe Y:** Automatically updates
- **View Graph:** Zoom out to see full trail

**Educational Concepts:**
- Functions as relationships (not just values)
- "Function" means "to serve a purpose"
- Independent vs. dependent variables
- Function notation: Y(X), Y = f(X)
- Graphing as visualization
- The Cartesian plane as reality

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Pass through narrow gate (main puzzle)
- Match Y's trail to target graph shape
- Create specific function to reach target point

---

#### **Stage 10: The Regular Polygon Path**
**Form:** Progressive vertex addition (Pentagon → Hexagon → Octagon)

**Mechanics:**
- Each added vertex grants new abilities
- Symmetry-based puzzles
- Rotational mechanics emphasized

**Pentagon (5 vertices):**
- 5-fold rotational symmetry
- Ability: Spin-dash (rotate while dashing)
- Puzzle: Fit into 5-pointed star slots

**Hexagon (6 vertices):**
- Perfect tessellation ability
- Ability: Tile with copies of self (visual effect)
- Puzzle: Navigate honeycomb structures

**Octagon (8 vertices):**
- Near-circular rolling
- Ability: Smooth rolling motion
- Puzzle: Roll down slopes efficiently

**Companion Voices:**
- Despite merging, Echo/Axiom/Sigma/Delta maintain distinct reactions
- Echo: "This is magnificent!"
- Axiom: "The pattern suggests we should..."
- Sigma: "We grow stronger together"
- Delta: "We are becoming"

**Educational Concepts:**
- Regular polygons (equal sides and angles)
- Interior angles
- Rotational symmetry
- Approaching the circle

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Rotate pentagon to align with 5 switches
- Tessellate hexagon pattern to fill gap
- Roll octagon through curved passage

---

#### **Stage 10b: Irregular Territories**
**Form:** Irregular polygons (deformable)

**Mechanics:**
- **Deformation System:** Drag vertices to create irregular shapes
- Concave shapes allowed
- Each irregular shape has unique uses

**Abilities:**
- **Concave Pocket:** Create pocket to carry objects
- **Irregular Fit:** Fit into irregular keyholes
- **Asymmetric Weight:** Tip in specific directions

**Educational Concepts:**
- Concave vs. convex
- Irregular shapes are valid
- Form follows function

**Puzzle Examples:**
- Deform into concave shape to scoop object
- Match irregular keyhole shape
- Create asymmetric weight distribution to tip platform

#### **Stage 11: The Circle's Infinity**
**Form:** Circle (infinite vertices)

**New Character:** Pi (π)

**Transformation:**
- Vertices blur into smooth curve
- Individual companion voices blend into harmonious hum
- Pi: "You haven't lost them. Infinity contains all things."

**Mechanics:**
- **Perfect Rolling:** No angular momentum loss
- **Spin-Drill:** Spin in place to drill through soft barriers
- **Infinite Smoothness:** Fit into any curved slot

**Heartlight Evolution:**
- Heartlight: "I'm becoming part of you. I am your insight."
- **New Ability:** Reveal hidden geometric properties (enhanced)
- See invisible paths, measure curves, calculate circumference

## 6. Educational Design

### 6.1 Pedagogical Approach

**Core Philosophy: "Feel It, Then Name It"**

**Traditional Math Education:**
```
Formula → Memorization → Application → (Maybe) Understanding
```

**Vector Zero's Approach:**
```
Experience → Pattern Recognition → Emotional Attachment → (Later) Formal Understanding
```

**Key Principles:**

1. **No Tutorials or Lectures**
   - Concepts emerge through gameplay
   - No explicit instruction
   - Discovery-based learning

2. **Mathematics as World Physics**
   - Not external knowledge
   - The game world IS mathematics
   - Characters discover truths within their reality

3. **Multiple Exposure Across Contexts**
   - Same concept appears in different forms

## 7. Puzzle Taxonomy

### 7.1 Geometric Puzzles

**Shape Formation:**
- Form specific polygon to fit keyhole
- Match number of vertices/edges/faces
- Create regular vs. irregular shapes

**Area/Volume:**
- Fill region with specific area
- Match volume requirement
- Calculate perimeter to unlock

**Structural Puzzles:**
- Build stable structures (rigid shapes)
- Support weight without collapsing
- Bridge gaps with appropriate forms

### 7.2 Variable Puzzles

**Assignment:**
- Set X = 5 to open gate
- Change Y to specific value
- Multiple assignment sequence (X=3, then X=7, then X=12)

**Comparison:**
- Make X > Y to unlock
- Ensure X < 10 to proceed
- Balance X and Y (X = Y)

**Nesting:**
- Put Y inside X (variable holding variable)
- Create expression X = (Y + Z)
- Unpack nested variables on other side

### 7.3 Equation Puzzles

**Balance Scales:**
- Adjust variables to balance equation
- Solve for unknown constant
- Multi-step equation solving

**Systems:**
- Two balance scales simultaneously
- Solve system of equations
- Consistent vs. inconsistent systems

**Equation Building:**
- Create equation matching target relationship
- Construct function from pieces
- Verify equation with specific values

### 7.4 Function Puzzles

**Graph Matching:**
- Create function whose trail matches target shape
- Match slope, intercept, curvature
- Linear, quadratic, exponential, etc.

**Composition:**
- Build f(g(x)) from separate functions
- Chain functions together
- Nested function creation

**Inverse Functions:**
- Reverse operations to undo function
- Create inverse function
- Demonstrate f(f⁻¹(x)) = x

**Domain/Range:**
- Navigate function only where defined
- Avoid undefined regions (e.g., tan 90°)
- Restricted domain puzzles

### 7.5 Transformation Puzzles

**Single Transformation:**
- Translate to target position
- Rotate to target orientation
- Reflect across specified axis
- Dilate to target size

**Compound Transformations:**
- Rotate 90°, then translate, then scale
- Order matters (non-commutative)
- Match target through sequence

**Inverse Transformations:**
- Undo transformation sequence
- Return to original state
- Find transformation that maps A → B

### 7.6 Trigonometry Puzzles

**Wave Riding:**
- Time jump to land on sine wave platform
- Navigate phase-shifted cosine platforms
- Ride multiple waves simultaneously

**Angle Calculation:**
- Position at specific angle to activate
- Predict wave height at given angle
- Use unit circle to navigate

**Slope Navigation:**
- Climb tangent ramps of varying steepness
- Avoid slopes approaching 90° (undefined)
- Calculate required angle for ramp

### 7.7 Calculus Puzzles

**Derivative Puzzles:**
- Move at specific velocity to read Differential's message
- Oscillate to create conversation
- Accelerate to required rate
- Project message forward to activate switch

**Limit Puzzles:**
- Approach target asymptotically (get close, never touch)
- Navigate smaller and smaller steps (Zeno's paradox)
- Zoom to infinitesimal scale

**Integral Puzzles:**
- Collect infinite slices to fill area
- Accumulate small changes to find total
- Reverse differentiation to find original function
- Switch Heartlight to integral mode

### 7.8 Calculus Puzzles (continued)

**Fundamental Theorem:**
- Demonstrate derivative undoes integral
- Accumulate rate of change to find position
- Differentiate accumulated area

### 7.9 3D Puzzles

**Spatial Navigation:**
- Navigate 3D mazes with vertical component
- Rotate in 3D to align with target
- Move in Z-axis to reach platforms

**Volume:**
- Fill 3D container with correct volume
- Match volumetric requirements
- Calculate volume to unlock

**Surface Area:**
- Match surface area specification
- Minimize/maximize surface area
- Surface area vs. volume relationships

**Cross-Sections:**
- View 2D slices of 3D objects
- Navigate through planes
- Understand 3D form from 2D views

### 7.10 Optimization Puzzles

**Gradient Following:**
- Follow gradient to reach maximum
- Oppose gradient to find minimum
- Navigate vector fields

**Saddle Points:**
- Navigate points that are min in one direction, max in another
- Recognize local vs. global extrema
- Multi-variable optimization

**Constrained Optimization:**
- Find optimum subject to constraints
- Balance multiple competing gradients
- Lagrange multiplier concepts (implicit)

### 7.11 Advanced Puzzles (Sub-Worlds)

**Probability:**
- Navigate probabilistic platforms
- Maximize chance of success
- Calculate expected values implicitly

**Set Theory:**
- Navigate Venn diagram regions
- Activate logic gates (AND, OR)
- Categorize elements

**Fractals:**
- Find self-similar patterns
- Zoom to reveal structure
- Identify generating rules

**Topology:**
- Navigate impossible surfaces
- Deform without tearing
- Recognize homeomorphic forms

### 7.12 Difficulty Progression

**Tutorial (Stages 1-3):**
- No failure possible
- Pure exploration
- Gentle introduction

**Learning (Stages 4-12):**
- Introduces challenge
- Infinite retries
- Hints available (optional)

**Mastery (Stages 13-20):**
- Significant challenge
- Timed elements (optional)
- Efficiency rewards

**Expert (Stages 21-24):**
- Complex multi-step puzzles
- Requires understanding of multiple concepts
- Optional super-challenges

**Optional:**
- Zero-death runs
- Speedrun modes
- Hidden ultra-difficult puzzles
- Sub-world master challenges

- **Low Poly Models:** Platonic solids are simple geometry
- **LOD (Level of Detail):** Reduce complexity at distance
- **Culling:** Frustum culling, occlusion culling
- **Lighting:** Minimal (mostly emissive materials)
- **Shaders:** Efficient glow/outline shaders

**General:**
- **Scene Management:** Unload unused assets
- **Audio:** Stream music, load SFX into memory
- **Resolution Scaling:** Allow lower resolution for performance
- **Target Framerate:** 60 FPS on recommended specs, 30 FPS minimum

## 13. Accessibility & Player Options

### 13.1 Core Accessibility Philosophy

**Inclusive Design:**
- Game should be playable by widest audience possible
- Accessibility integrated, not added late
- Respectful of diverse abilities and needs

**No Punishment:**
- Infinite retries (always)
- No fail states (except optional challenges)
- Save anywhere/anytime

### 13.2 Visual Accessibility

**Colorblind Modes:**
- Protanopia (red-blind)
- Deuteranopia (green-blind)
- Tritanopia (blue-blind)
- Achromatopsia (full colorblind)
- Remaps all color-coded elements

**Contrast & Brightness:**
- High contrast mode
- Adjustable overall brightness
- Outline thickness adjustable
- Disable bloom/glow (reduce visual noise)

**UI Scaling:**
- UI size: Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large
- Font size: independent control
- HUD element customization (position, size, visibility)

**Photosensitivity:**
- No rapid flashing
- Disable screen shake
- Disable rapid color changes
- Warning before intense visuals

### 13.3 Audio Accessibility

**Visual Alternatives:**
- **Subtitles:** All dialogue, adjustable size/color/background
- **Closed Captions:** Include sound effect descriptions
- **Visual Sound Indicators:** Directional arrows for off-screen sounds
- **Screen Flashes:** Visual cue for important audio

**Audio Controls:**
- Music, SFX, Voice volumes independent
- Mono audio (for single-ear hearing)
- Audio balance (L/R adjustment)
- Mute all audio (visual-only mode)

**Audio Descriptions (Experimental):**
- Narration of visual elements for blind players
- Complex feature, may be post-release

### 13.4 Motor/Input Accessibility

**Control Simplification:**
- Combine actions (e.g., auto-dash on collision with gap)
- Toggle vs. hold options (Endpoint Mode: toggle or hold)
- Reduce rapid inputs required
- No quick-time events

**Customizable Controls:**
- Full rebinding
- Multiple control schemes
- Sensitivity adjustments (movement, camera)
- Gamepad vibration toggle

**Assistive Features:**
- **Auto-aim:** Snap to interactive objects
- **Slow-motion:** Reduce game speed for timing puzzles
- **Infinite time:** Disable timers (if any)
- **Auto-complete:** Option to skip particularly difficult puzzles (last resort)

### 13.5 Cognitive Accessibility

**Difficulty Options:**
- **Hints:** Toggle on/off, frequency adjustable
- **Puzzle Complexity:** Option to simplify some puzzles
- **Navigation Assistance:** Highlight next objective
- **Memory Aids:** Journal showing discovered concepts

**Clear Communication:**
- Simple language
- Avoid jargon (outside mathematical terms, which are the point)
- Visual cues supplement text
- Consistent iconography

**Pacing:**
- Player-controlled pacing (no forced rushing)
- Save anywhere
- Pause anytime

### 13.6 Neurodiversity Support

**Sensory Control:**
- Reduce visual noise (particle effects)
- Reduce audio noise (ambient sounds)
- Simplify backgrounds
- Disable animations (optional)

**Focus Assistance:**
- Highlight current objective
- Reduce distractions
- Clear visual hierarchy

**Overstimulation Prevention:**
- Calm mode (reduce all sensory input)
- Breaks encouraged (auto-pause after X minutes, optional)

### 13.7 Language & Localization

**Initial Release:**
- English (primary)

**Future:**
- Major languages (Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, etc.)
- Community translation support

**Text Accessibility:**
- Dyslexic-friendly fonts (OpenDyslexic option)
- Adjustable text speed (if text auto-scrolls)
- Clear, readable fonts

### 13.8 Assist Mode (Comprehensive)

**Optional "Assist Mode" Toggle:**
- Enables multiple assists simultaneously
- Not a "cheat"—a legitimate way to experience the game
- Includes:
  - Hints always on
  - Slow-motion available
  - Auto-aim for interactions

  - No fail states (even more forgiving)
  - Skip puzzle option (last resort, with confirmation)

**Philosophy:**
- Everyone deserves to experience the story
- Learning happens even with assists
- No shame in using assists
